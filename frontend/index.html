About KKMMPTC Kallettumkara

Welcome to K. Karunakaran Memorial Model Polytechnic College, Kallettumkara

Established in 1993 under the aegis of the Institute of Human Resources Development (IHRD), an autonomous institution of the Government of Kerala, K. Karunakaran Memorial Model Polytechnic College, Kallettumkara stands as a premier beacon of technical education in Thrissur district.

Affiliated with the Board of Technical Education, Kerala, and approved by AICTE, New Delhi, the institution offers regular 3-year diploma programs in cutting-edge branches including Electronics Engineering, Bio-Medical Engineering, Computer Hardware Engineering, Computer Engineering, Electrical & Electronics, and Robotic Process Automation.

"Creating professionals with excellent technical skills and social commitment"

About Technical Knowledge:

To cultivate a community of professionals driven by a passion for technical excellence through comprehensive and cutting-edge education, mentorship, and experiential learning.

About Social Commitment:

Promoting the true spirit of social commitment by accomplishing community development activities.

Founded in 1993 at Kallettumkara, Thrissur, the college was established to cater to the growing demand for technical manpower in Kerala. Renamed in memory of former Chief Minister K. Karunakaran, the college has continuously modernized its infrastructure across its 12.375-acre campus.

IHRD Governance & Management

Governed by Executive Committee chaired by Hon'ble Minister for Higher Education, Govt. of Kerala.
